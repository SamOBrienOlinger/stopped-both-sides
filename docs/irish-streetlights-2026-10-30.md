# Irish streetlight replacements · 30 October 2026

Replaces the free-standing three-lantern streetlights in all three outdoor encounter backgrounds and both homepage perspectives with a miniature interpretation of a documented Dublin swan-neck standard. The figures, scenario directions, layout and gameplay are unchanged. The Ha'penny Bridge retains its own white bridge lights.

## Reference

National Inventory of Architectural Heritage, [Mountshannon Road, Kilmainham, Dublin 8, record 50080197](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50080197/mountshannon-road-kilmainham-dublin-8-co-dublin). The record describes a fluted column, swan-neck, scrolls and a shamrock motif. [Detail photograph](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50080197_2.jpg) and [full standard](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50080197_1.jpg) guided the shape. Reference photographs are not included in production assets.

These remain fictional miniature scenes, not surveyed reconstructions of specific lamp positions.

## Production

Used the built-in image-generation tool in precise-object-edit mode, then Sharp only for responsive sizing and WebP encoding. The three scene backgrounds retain 1280 × 716 dimensions. Both hero illustrations retain 480, 960 and 1600-pixel responsive variants at 16:9. Updated asset and importing-module query versions refresh previously cached artwork.

Final assets:

- `assets/scenes/street.webp`
- `assets/scenes/bus-stop.webp`
- `assets/scenes/square.webp`
- `assets/heroes/public-{480,960,1600}.webp`
- `assets/heroes/garda-{480,960,1600}.webp`
