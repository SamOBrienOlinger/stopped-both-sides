Reference image is STYLE ONLY, its detailed molded LEGO-like Dublin streetscape, warm muted teal, ochre, navy palette and eye-level camera. Create a 3-column by 2-row atlas of SIX DIFFERENT empty locations, equal exact rectangular 16:9 cells, full canvas ratio 8:3 (e.g. 3072x1152). No gutters, no borders, no labels, absolutely NO people or figures in any cell. Each panel must have a broad EMPTY unobstructed foreground floor occupying bottom 45%, level and in perspective, for two full-body toy figures standing facing each other. Keep furniture and details in the back half or edges, never in foreground. All six consistent toy scale, camera at toy chest height, beautiful realistic plastic brick construction, soft cinematic lighting. Top-left: Dublin street outside a closed red-brick shop, phone-booth style shopfront details, pavement, evening warm windows. Top-middle: Dublin bus stop shelter and distant yellow double-decker bus on street, pavement foreground, overcast daytime. Top-right: calm Garda station reception with a teal counter along BACK wall, clock and pale neutral walls, broad empty cream-tiled foreground. Bottom-left: private quiet advice room with small table against BACK wall, window and bookshelf, soft natural daylight, empty foreground. Bottom-middle: community meeting/review room with teal chairs and pinboard along BACK wall and a screen with abstract non-text non-data blocks, broad empty wood-floor foreground. Bottom-right: quiet outdoor public square with café frontage at the edges, brick buildings and planter in back, broad clear stone pavement foreground. NO readable letters or numbers, NO legal symbols, NO guns, NO prison bars, NO people, NO characters. All six are complete independent backgrounds and their exact equal cell borders matter. Rich depth, gentle distance blur, no blurry foreground. The supplied image's CHARACTERS MUST BE REMOVED and are not part of the requested atlas.
