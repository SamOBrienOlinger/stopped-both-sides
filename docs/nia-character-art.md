# Nia: additional public character

Created 23 November 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool.

## Character and integration

Nia is a fictional Black adult woman and the tenth selectable member of the public (`public-10`). Her warm brown complexion, natural black curls, plum jacket, cream top and dark teal trousers distinguish her within the existing toy-figure roster. Existing character names, IDs, artwork, preferences and saved progress are preserved.

The original generated RGBA PNG is saved without image edits at `assets/characters/nia-public-20261123.png` (1254 × 1254 pixels). It is reused for the picker, active-role headshot, cast cards, character profile and scene figure. The game currently uses a neutral stance throughout decisions, feedback and recaps, so Nia follows the same behavior. `encounters/public-artwork.mjs` centres the complete figure and aligns the measured soles to the existing 97.5% scene floor using CSS; the transparent source pixels remain unchanged.

The generation uses a standalone new asset. Neither the original 18-character sheet nor the current Garda atlas was altered. Nia is eligible for the situations requiring a woman; the older-person restriction continues to use its existing age-appropriate characters. The dedicated supporting person in the colleague/race situation remains separate from the playable public witness.

Make her clearly an adult woman and a member of the public in everyday contemporary Irish city clothing. Standing upright, nearly front-facing with only a very slight turn toward viewer right, both arms relaxed, both hands and both feet fully visible. A single square canvas, genuine alpha-transparent background. Centre the complete figure horizontally; top of hair at approximately 7% of canvas height and soles at approximately 96%, with ample transparent side margins. Consistent soft neutral studio lighting and clean readable silhouette, no opaque floor or cast-shadow panel. Output exactly ONE whole figure, no grid, no second view, no environment, no inset, no name, no labels, no text, no logos, no watermark, no police uniform or equipment.
