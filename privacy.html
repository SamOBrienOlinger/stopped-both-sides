Privacy note · 11 November 2026

Your learning and your information

Stopped: Both Sides is an independent educational project by Sam O’Brien-Olinger, Sam Tim Solutions, Ireland. For privacy questions, corrections or personal-information requests, email samobrienolinger@gmail.com.

This game is not a reporting service, legal advice channel or police-record system. Do not use it to submit names, allegations, immigration details or sensitive information about a real encounter. The characters and learning situations are fictional.

Local progress and settings

The game keeps fictional choices, scores and a resume point in your browser. Reading and accessibility controls can also remember your selected preferences. No account is required to play. Review or clear your learning record under Scores & progress or the Garda progress view. Clearing all browser site data can also remove preferences; because GitHub Pages projects can share an origin, this can affect other projects hosted there.

Locally retained records remain until cleared, replaced or removed by browser storage management; the current game does not promise a fixed automatic deletion date. People using the same browser profile may be able to see saved learning records. Separate browsers do not automatically synchronise them.

Learning records in links

The companion-perspective link can carry a learning record in the link to continue the experience. Treat a copied learning link as shareable data: someone receiving it may be able to read or import the included record. Browser history, bookmarks or a shared screenshot can retain a link. Do not put real incident information into a fictional learning record, and clear records before using a shared device.

Hosting and external resources

GitHub Pages processes technical requests, including connection information such as an IP address and the requested resource, to deliver and secure the site. These hosting records are different from the local learning record. See GitHub's privacy statement for its processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements.

Source links lead to independent organisations and their privacy policies apply when you visit them. This update does not introduce analytics, advertising pixels or a complaint-collection form. Adding a new embedded or tracking service would require a fresh review and, where required, prior consent rather than relying on this note.

Contact and your rights

Email you choose to send includes your address, message and any attachments. It is used to answer the enquiry and manage related issues, based on the legitimate interest in responding to people who contact the project. Send only necessary information. Retention depends on resolving the enquiry, maintaining a necessary record and any applicable legal requirement; ask about the position for a specific record.

Where the project processes personal information, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to legitimate-interest processing. Rights have conditions and exceptions. Contact the email above; the usual GDPR response period is one month, subject to permitted extensions and proportionate identity checks. You may complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

General information, not legal advice. Not endorsed by any organisation or An Garda Síochána.
